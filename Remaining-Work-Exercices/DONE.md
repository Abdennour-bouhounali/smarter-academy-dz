# What was done (2026-10-06 → 2026-10-07)

## 1. Step 0 — index of the BEM annals 2007 → 2025

- `docs/sources/4am/bem-practice/annals-index.md` (+ `.json`): the whole corpus, read page by page from the
  rendered PDFs (`pdftoppm -r 150`): **19 sessions, 95 exercises** (4 exercises + الجزء الثاني per session).
- For each exercise: session, exercise, points (ن), page in the exam PDF, pages of its official solution, topic,
  the ONE lesson that cites it, the lesson of each step, out-of-lesson steps (`counted:false`), mixed exercises,
  الجزء الثاني problems.
- Per-lesson table: which annals exercises each lesson owns. **32 lessons own no annals exercise** (all of unit 8
  الإحصاء, all of unit 14 الفضاء, regular polygons, most of unit 1).
- The index is regenerated by `tools/build_index.py`; corrections found during the rollout are recorded in it
  (see §5).

## 3. Owner decisions applied

- Banks **replaced in place, ids kept** (existing exercises → `kind: adapted`); no DB migration.
- Lesson with no annals → « على منوال BEM <year> » + adapted only; label kept even when only the structure of the
  session is reused.
- Steps of another lesson / earlier year stay as printed with `counted: false`.
- Level 5 = 10 NEW « الوضعية الإدماجية (08 ن) »; the lesson's « مسائل » modules stay untouched.
- Photos + teacher validation in every converted lesson. Free/premium unchanged.
- Order: pilot unit 2, then ONE LESSON AT A TIME from the oldest lesson on the platform (unit 1, then 3 → 14).
- bahth-pgcd level 5 aligned (all 10 are وضعيات إدماجية).
