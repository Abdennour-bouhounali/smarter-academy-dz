## 5. Design

**The lesson in one paragraph.** At the village's olive-oil cooperative, two stainless tanks of the same size stand side by side. Each
has a glass sight tube on its side, but the technicians graduated them differently: the left tube in quarters, the right one in twelfths.
The delivery slips say: left « $\frac34$ خزّان », right « $\frac7{12}$ خزّان ». Which tank holds more? Anis slides the laser level up its
rail to the left tank's oil line: the red line crosses the right tube at the 9th mark — **3/4 is 9/12** — and the right oil (7 marks) is
BELOW the laser: 7/12 < 9/12 = 3/4. With two tubes in the same parts (eighths), the laser shows at once that the one with more marks is
higher. With the same number of marks filled (3 of 5 vs 3 of 8), the tube cut in more parts is lower: its marks are smaller. A tank above
its « 1 » line (9/8) is over-full — every fraction below 1 is below it.

**Entry point — candidates compared:**
- (a) **The book's act-6** (drawn loaves): the drawing does the comparing; the student only reads it. Rejected → M4.
- (b) **A number line** (place both fractions): placing is 1AM `hasil-qisma-mustaqim` (planned) and 3AM `number-line-pegs`.
- (c) **Colour mixing / dispensing** (match a shade): 3AM `shade-match` (the successor lesson's lab).
- (d) **Two tanks with differently graduated sight tubes, a laser level the hand slides**: the laser makes ONE height readable in BOTH
  graduations — the rewriting 3/4 = 9/12 is SEEN, not computed. **Chosen.**
  `experience: { type: 'platform_context', reason: 'platform_designed_entry', theme: 'oilpress' }`.

**Order of ideas:** same graduation (P1) → one graduation a multiple of the other (P3) → same number of marks, different graduations (P2)
→ the « 1 » line (P4) → three tanks in order (P3) → معارف → طريقة (rewrite, order, via 1, decimal numerators) → practice, مسائل, test.

**Contexts (values):** the olive cooperative (working together, local products), class donations for the school library (generosity),
reading time (perseverance), a farm's land (stewardship).

## 6. Lab — `level-laser` « ليزر المستوى » (new type)

**Drawn world `oilpress`** (new theme, kind `levels`, label « معصرة الزيتون التعاونية: خزّانات بأنابيب زجاجية مدرّجة وليزر مستوى »):
- a stone-walled press room; two (at متقدم three) identical stainless tanks on legs; on each tank a vertical glass sight tube showing the
  golden oil level; each tube graduated with its OWN number of equal marks (b), labelled 0 and 1 only (the marks are plain ticks; the
  slip on the tank says the fraction);
- a « 1 » line (the brim) drawn across the tanks; above it, a small reserve tube for the over-full case (the level continues);
- a laser level on a vertical rail between the tanks: a red line across every sight tube, with a small readout chip on EACH tube where the
  line crosses it: « على العلامة 9 من 12 » or « بين العلامتين 4 و 5 من 8 »;
- at متقدم the tubes are covered by a canvas sleeve (lifted after the prediction);
- KidFigure Anis (boy, hoodie) at the rail at مبتدئ; Salma (girl, hijab + tunic, apron) at متقدم.

**The variable in the hand: the laser's HEIGHT h** (as a fraction of the tank: snaps to the marks of BOTH tubes — the union of the two
graduations; between two marks of the other tube the chip says « بين »).

**Gestures (three paths):** drag the laser's carriage up/down the rail; ↑/↓ (next mark of the union), PageUp/PageDown (next mark of the
coarser tube); « اضبط على خزّان » buttons that snap the laser to a tank's oil line (they are offered only AFTER the student has dragged once —
the hand comes first); then the comparison question.

**The question and its judge:** « أيّ الخزّانين فيه زيت أكثر؟ » (choose the tank or « متساويان ») AND « اكتب كسر الخزّان الأيسر بأجزاء
الخزّان الأيمن » when one graduation is a multiple of the other (typed: 9/12) — judged from the logic; refused models: `numOnly` (compares
numerators across different denominators: 7 > 3 so 7/12 > 3/4), `bigDen` (more marks = more oil: 3/8 > 3/5), `noScale` (3/4 = 3/12),
`gap` (« both are one part short of full »: 3/4 = 7/8?). A refused choice is answered by the WORLD: the laser jumps to the claimed tank's
level and the other tank's oil is visibly on the wrong side of the red line.

**Opening (no title):**
- Context: « في معصرة التعاونية خزّانان متماثلان. ورقة الخزّان الأيسر: $\frac34$ ، وورقة الأيمن: $\frac7{12}$ . »
- Question: « حرّك الليزر حتى يلمس زيت الخزّان الأيسر، واقرأ أين يقطع أنبوب الخزّان الأيمن، ثم قل أيّ الخزّانين فيه زيت أكثر. »
- `lead`: « الخطّ الأحمر في نفس الارتفاع على الخزّانين. »

**M5 مسألة 1 (`lessonProblem`) — « تبرّعات الأقسام »** — `objectives`: « أقارن حصصًا وأجد الباقي ».
- Statement: « جمعت ثلاثة أقسام تبرّعات لمكتبة المدرسة: القسم الأوّل $\frac5{12}$ من المبلغ، والثاني $\frac13$ منه، والثالث الباقي. »
- Q/A: 1) حصّة الثالث → $1-\frac5{12}-\frac4{12}=\frac3{12}=\frac14$ ; 2) رتّب الحصص تنازليًا → $\frac5{12}>\frac4{12}>\frac3{12}$ ;
  3) « هل ساهم القسم الأوّل بأكثر من النصف؟ » → لا ($\frac5{12}<\frac6{12}$).

**M6 مسألة 2 — « من قرأ أكثر؟ »** — `objectives`: « أقارن كسرين لهما نفس البسط ».

  → ياسر وهدى.
