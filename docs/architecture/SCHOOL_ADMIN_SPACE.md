# « فضاء الإدارة » — the school administration space

> Status (2026-10-26): **Phases 0–4 implemented** — contract, data model, the
> `school_admin` role and its gate, the privacy gate `SchoolAccess`, the
> platform admin's « المؤسسات », the school's STRUCTURE (terms, teachers,
> classes, students, roster import, login cards) and its TRACKING (the
> الكفاءات map per class, the student file). Phases 5–7 (reports, meeting view,
> licence) are `Planned`. The code is the source of truth.

A **school** (« المؤسسة », a متوسطة) is a first-class object: its classes, its
teachers, its students, and one **director** (« المدير ») who manages them,
follows their learning, prints reports and projects them in meetings. This is
« Model C » of `TEACHER_CLASSROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.

## 0. Owner decisions (2026-10-26)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | **The school buys a licence** of student seats; its students get full access through it; a teacher's own seats are not consumed by a covered school class. (`Planned` — phase 7. Until then a school class uses its TEACHER's seats.) |
| D2 | **The director may enrol a student directly** (imported roster → class). The school's consent is recorded at enrolment (`school_consent_by = school`); the student confirms the teacher's sharing text once in « قسمي » (non-blocking). Built (phase 3). |
| D3 | **One school role: the director.** `school_staff.school_role` exists so a second role is a value, not a migration. |
| D4 | **Teacher activity is shown as FACTS** — never a score, never a ranking — and **the teacher sees exactly what the school sees** about them. Built (phase 3): ONE function `TeacherActivityFacts::for()` and ONE component `TeacherFacts.jsx` serve both readers. |
| D5 | **A student without an e-mail gets a generated « معرّف الدخول »** (`s{schoolId}.{6 chars}@eleve.smarter-academy.com`, domain `config('school.student_login_domain')`), verified at creation, printed on a login card with a password shown once. No mail is ever sent to it (`User::routeNotificationForMail()` returns `null`); a lost password is re-issued on a card. (Owner, 2026-10-26.) |

## 2. Who exists — one more role

```text
role          registers via                          lands on   gate
school_admin  created by the platform admin only     /idara     can:school
```

- `school_admin` is **not** self-registerable and is **not** `admin`: a director
  never passes `can:admin`, an admin never passes `can:school`.
- Three authorization layers on every `/school` request:

```text
role         Gate 'school'                     route group can:school → 403 for admin, teacher, student, parent
membership   SchoolAccess::schoolOf            an ACTIVE school_staff row on an ACTIVE school → 403 reason no_school
entitlement  the school's licence              Planned (phase 7)
```

- A director belongs to **one** school (`school_staff.user_id` is unique). The
  school is never read from the request: every `/school` controller extends
  `School\SchoolController`, whose `withSchool()` resolves it from the director
  and maps refusals (`no_school` → 403, every other reason → 422 `{reason}`).
- The director's e-mail is marked verified at creation; `email.verified` still guards the routes.

## 3. Data model

| Table / column | Key columns | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `schools` | `name`, `wilaya`, `commune`, `code` (unique, optional), **`teacher_join_token`** (32 base62, unique, born with the school), `status` `active\|suspended\|archived`, `current_academic_year` | created and edited by the platform admin only; suspending closes `/school` (`no_school`) and touches no class or student |
| `school_staff` | `school_id`, `user_id` (**unique**), `school_role` (`director`), `status` `active\|revoked`, `appointed_by`, `appointed_at`, `revoked_at` | revoking keeps the row and the account and deletes the director's tokens |
| `school_teachers` | `school_id`, `teacher_id`, `status` `active\|left`, `joined_at`, `left_at`; unique `(school_id, teacher_id)` | **one active school per teacher** (`already_in_school`) |
| `school_terms` | `school_id`, `academic_year`, `term` 1–3, `starts_on`, `ends_on`; unique `(school_id, academic_year, term)` | set as ONE block of three ordered, non-overlapping terms (`invalid_terms`) |
| `classrooms.school_id` | nullable FK | a class OF a school; the owner stays `teacher_id` (§4) |
| `classroom_memberships.school_consent_at` / `school_consent_by` (`student\|school`) | the consent that lets the SCHOOL read this student | null = the school neither reads nor counts the student |
| `classroom_memberships.joined_via = school_enrolment` | a student enrolled by the director (D2) | |
| `users.birth_date` | date, set only for a student imported by the school | used ONLY to recognise a namesake at import; never shown, never logged |

## 4. Privacy — the school is a NEW reader

The teacher contract (`TEACHER_CLASSROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2 « Consent and what
the teacher may read ») stays as it is. The school adds one gate of its own.

**`Domain\School\SchoolAccess`** — the ONE school → student gate and the ONE
school-facing identity:

- `schoolOf($user)`: the director's active school, otherwise `no_school` → 403.
- `consentedMemberships($school)`: active memberships in ACTIVE classes of the
  school WITH `school_consent_at` — every school aggregate starts here.
- `activeStudent($school, $studentId)`: such a membership exists, otherwise **404**.
- `identity()`: `{id, firstName, lastName}` — **never the e-mail**.

**How the school's consent is given** (`ClassroomMembershipService::admit`, the
one write of a membership):

| Way in | School consent |
|---|---|
| the class **link** of a school class (`/rejoindre/classe/{token}`) | `student` — the link page shows the same sharing text PLUS « هذا القسم تابع لمؤسسة … » (`SharingDisclosure schoolName`, preview field `schoolName`); the same gesture accepts both. A visitor's pending join, completed after e-mail verification, goes through the link too |
| the class **code** (« قسمي ») | **none** — the code form cannot show the school before the gesture; « قسمي » asks afterwards |
| **enrolment by the director** (`enrolBySchool`, D2) | `school`; the teacher's sharing text stays unconsented (`consented_at` null) and « قسمي » asks it, without blocking |
| a class **attached** later by its teacher | none for the existing members: each one is asked in « قسمي » |

- « قسمي » shows one card per class with `studyContext.classrooms[].schoolConsentRequired`
  (a school class whose membership was not consented BY THE STUDENT); « أوافق »
  calls `POST /student/classrooms/{id}/school-consent` (`school_consent_by = student`,
  log `classroom_member.school_consented`).
- **Detaching** a class (teacher) or a teacher leaving / being detached clears
  `school_consent_*` on that class's memberships and `classrooms.school_id`: another
  school must ask again. Nothing else is deleted.

**Classes stay owned by their teacher.** `school_id` adds a reader, never a
second owner. `ClassroomPolicy` is unchanged.

**No averages.** Every school figure about learning is a **distribution**
(counts), never a mean (`TEACHER_CLASSROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md` §4, 2026-09-29).

**Locked by `SchoolPrivacyContractTest`**: every `/school` read carries no `email`
key and no student address at any depth (a generated login travels as `login`,
and only for a school-created account); every `/school` route answers 403 to an
admin, a teacher, a student and a parent, and to a revoked director or a
suspended school; a non-consented member is neither counted nor listed.
**Every new `/school` read is added to `schoolReads()` in that test.**

## 6. The director's space `/idara` (Arabic, RTL)

Tablet and laptop (root `CLAUDE.md`), plus the projector for the meeting view.
Lazy routes, `SchoolLayout`, namespace `idara` on demand. Shared look and helpers:
`features/school/ui.js`.

```text
/idara             لوحة المؤسسة   counts (classes, teachers, consented students)                      built
/idara/aqsam       الأقسام        classes: members vs « يطّلع عليهم المدير », create for a teacher, reassign  built
/idara/asatidha    الأساتذة       join link (copy, renew), each teacher with TeacherFacts, detach       built
/idara/talamidh    التلاميذ       consented students per class, roster import, transfer, login cards   built
/idara/iidadat     الإعدادات      the three terms                                                       built
/idara/talamidh/:id ملف التلميذ   the parent's « التقدّم بالتفصيل » (ProgressDetail audience="school")   built (phase 4)
/idara/kafaat      الكفاءات       each الكفاءة of a grade, class by class, as counts                    built (phase 4)
/idara/taqarir     التقارير       catalogue, snapshots, print, export                                   Planned (phase 5)
/idara/ijtimaa/:id وضع الاجتماع   projection, « إخفاء الأسماء »                                        Planned (phase 6)
```

**Classes** (`SchoolClassroomService`). The director creates a class FOR a
teacher of the school through `ClassroomService::create` (the teacher's plan
rules apply: `grade_not_allowed`, `limit_reached`) and gives it `school_id`;
the year defaults to the school's. **Reassigning** to another teacher of the
school checks that teacher's free seats for the class's not-yet-seated students
(`seats_exhausted`, until the licence of phase 7).

**Teachers** (`SchoolTeacherService`). The director sends the link
`/enseignant/muassasa/rejoindre/{teacher_join_token}` (the server returns the
token only); renewing it kills the old one (`invalid_link`). **Detaching** a
teacher ends the row and gives the classes back (§4).

**Students** (`SchoolRosterService`):

- **Import in two steps on the same rows** (max `config('school.roster_max_rows')` = 60):
  the client parses a pasted list or a CSV (`features/school/rosterCsv.js`, pure,
  tested: comma / semicolon / tab, Arabic or French header skipped, `14/03/2012` →
  `2012-03-14`); `POST /school/roster/preview` creates nothing and returns each row's
  status — `new`, `exists` (same first name + last name + birth date among the
  students the school already reads), `duplicate_in_file`, `invalid` with its
  `problem` (`firstName_not_arabic`, `birthDate_invalid`, `birthDate_out_of_range`
  (8–20 years)…) — plus the class teacher's `seatsRemaining`.
  `POST /school/roster` creates **all or nothing**: an invalid row refuses the whole
  import (`invalid_roster`), missing seats roll everything back (`seats_exhausted`).
  Each new student: role `student`, the class's grade, `birth_date`, a generated
  « معرّف الدخول » (D5), verified, enrolled by `enrolBySchool`. The response
  (`no-store`) carries the cards — the only time the passwords exist in clear.
  Log `school.roster_imported`: ids and counts, never a password or a birth date.
- **Transfer** between two classes of the school: the old membership is closed
  as `removed` by the director (not as the student's own departure), the director
  enrols in the new one; a « yes » the student gave the school follows them.
- **Login cards** (`POST /school/students/credentials`): new passwords, shown
  once, **only for school-created accounts** — a personal account's password
  belongs to its student (`not_school_login`). Printed with the teacher's
  « بطاقات الدخول » sheet (`features/teacher/members/credentials.css`,
  `features/school/SchoolCredentialCards.jsx`).

API (`auth:sanctum` + `account.active` + `email.verified` + `can:school`):

```text
GET  /school/overview
GET  /school/terms[?year=]           PUT /school/terms {academicYear, terms[3]}
GET  /school/teachers                → {joinLinkToken, teachers[{id, firstName, lastName, joinedAt, facts}]}
POST /school/teachers/join-link/rotate      DELETE /school/teachers/{teacherId}
GET  /school/classrooms              → {classrooms[{id, name, grade, academicYear, teacher, membersCount, readableCount}]}
POST /school/classrooms {name, grade, academicYear?, teacherId}
PATCH /school/classrooms/{id}/teacher {teacherId}
GET  /school/students[?classroom=]   → {students[{id, firstName, lastName, classroom, login|null, enrolledBySchool}]}
POST /school/roster/preview {classroomId, rows[]}     POST /school/roster {classroomId, rows[]}  (no-store)
PATCH /school/students/{studentId}/classroom {classroomId}
POST /school/students/credentials {studentIds[]}  (no-store)
GET  /school/learning-map[?grade=]   → {grades[], map{grade, classes[{id, name, students}], lessons[{code, title, learningPoints[{code, title, byClass{classId: counts}, total: counts, difficultyScope}]}]}}
GET  /school/students/{studentId}/progress   → {student{id, firstName, lastName, classroom}, progress}  (404 outside the school)
```

**Tracking (phase 4, `SchoolProgressService`).** It recomputes nothing: it READS
the canonical Mastery V2 state (`student_learning_point_progress`) on the
students the school may read (`consentedMemberships`).

- **The الكفاءات map** of a grade: the learning points of the lessons at least one
  class of that grade sees (`ClassroomProgressService::effectiveLessonIds`, so a
  lesson hidden by every teacher is absent), and for each point and each class
  the **counts** `{mastered, reinforce, gap, unassessed}` (unassessed = readable
  students without a state), a `total` over the classes, and `difficultyScope`
  (`LearningTrend::difficultyScope`, the teacher's indicator) over the total.
  Three grouped queries whatever the school's size. **No average, no
  percentage per class** — the page draws each cell as a bar of counts with the
  numbers under it, in the mastery colours of `ProgressDetail`.
- **The student file** is `ParentProgressService::forStudent` — the SAME payload
  and the SAME component (`ProgressDetail`, `audience="school"`, sentence
  `family:progress.supportIntroSchool`) the student and the guardian read
  (`PARENT_SPACE.md` §4bis). **Study time is NOT shown to the school**: the text
  the student accepts names progress and الكفاءات, not hours.
- Locked by `SchoolProgressTest` (the map equals the engine's rows; a
  non-consented student is absent; no `average`/`mean` key) and the two reads in
  `SchoolPrivacyContractTest`.

## 7. The teacher's side — « مؤسستي » (D4)

`/enseignant/muassasa` (sidebar « مؤسستي ») and the join page
`/enseignant/muassasa/rejoindre/:token`, strings in `idara › teacher.*`.

- **Opening the link joins nothing**: the page lists what the school will see
  (`teacher.join.items.*`: classes, consented students, interventions opened and
  closed over 30 days, last sign-in) and « أوافق وأنضمّ » is the gesture
  (`consent_required` without it).
- « مؤسستي » shows the SAME `TeacherFacts` component the director sees, the
  teacher's classes with « ربط بالمؤسسة / فكّ الربط », and « مغادرة المؤسسة ».
- `lastSignInAt` is `users.last_activity_at`, written at login only — the fact is
  named « آخر دخول » for that reason.

```text
GET  /teacher/school                 → {school|null, facts|null}
GET  /teacher/school-links/{token}   → {schoolLink{id, name, wilaya, commune, alreadyMember, currentSchoolName}}
POST /teacher/school/join {token, consent}       POST /teacher/school/leave
PUT  /teacher/classrooms/{id}/school {attached}  (ClassroomPolicy manage)
```

The teacher's class shape gains `schoolId`.
